# Reviewed bar stock confirmation

The hosted function inspection and a disposable native PostgreSQL reproduction found a saved draft reviewed as 2 cartons of 24 units could add only 24 units after the product changed to 12 units/carton. The forward migration replaces only the existing confirmation wrapper; it does not rewrite any products, prices, stock, drafts, accounts or historical audit rows.

New confirmations lock products in stable ID order before balances, recheck current permission after lock waits, and compare active status, product name, carton size, per-line units and total against the saved draft. A mismatch rejects the whole batch before any addition or cost update. The worker closes the review, cancels the stale draft with a reason and manually enters the actual quantities using current setup. A confirmed draft still returns its original proof on retry after later product edits. The internal engine remains inaccessible to browser roles.

The normal worker workflow is unchanged: enter quantities, Save & Review, then Final Confirm. Phone review displays full product names, wraps quantity explanations, labels cost per individual unit and keeps 44px actions visible. It does not copy quantities into a new batch.

Before sending final confirmation, the browser retains the original draft snapshot and actor under a project/account-scoped recovery key. A tab lock, compare-and-set storage checks and immediate busy guard prevent competing replacements or duplicate taps. Unknown, malformed, rejected and timed-out replies are not a success message. A connection failure retains the draft and offers Check Same Confirmation; reload restores it without automatically submitting, while replacement entry and cancellation remain blocked until resolved. Definitive first-request rejection can release only that exact pending attempt; later uncertain attempts are retained. Corrupt or inaccessible storage fails closed. Late replies and unmounted views cannot close a newer review.

Canonical unit tests cover reply identity, quantities, costs, malformed input, storage errors, actor/project separation, exact retained payloads, tab exclusion and deadlines. The native test reproduces the old issue, rehearses a record-preserving upgrade/replay, verifies multi-item rollback, simultaneous confirmation and both orderings of product-edit races, and checks revoked permissions after a wait. Controlled browser tests cover English/Malay phone/tablet/desktop review, full names, error recovery, a late reply, reload without submission and one stock addition after retry. These tests use isolated records, not real receipts or physical opening stock. Hosted verification remains read-only; actual opening balances and a real two-device pilot still require business acceptance.
